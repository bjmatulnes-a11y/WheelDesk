# Compliance and Risk Notes

## Positioning
WheelDesk should be positioned as **analytics, education, and workflow decision support** — not personalized investment advice.

## Claims to avoid
- "Guaranteed income"
- "Safe returns"
- "Beat the market"
- "Best trade" or certainty language

## Preferred language
- "Candidate contracts"
- "Scenario-based outcomes"
- "Rule-driven ranking"
- "For educational and informational purposes"

## Required disclosures
- Options involve risk, including loss of principal.
- Assignment may occur at any time for short American-style contracts.
- Historical performance does not guarantee future results.
- Users are responsible for suitability and execution decisions.

## Legal review required before launch
1. Marketing website copy and paid ad claims.
2. Onboarding questionnaire wording.
3. Alert/report message templates.
4. Terms of Service + Privacy Policy + Disclaimer language.
5. Whether any feature crosses into adviser-like personalization based on jurisdiction.

## Product guardrails
- No one-click execution in MVP.
- No account linking required for core use.
- Recommendation cards include assumptions and uncertainty ranges.
- In-app reminders before earnings/dividend events and concentration risk.
